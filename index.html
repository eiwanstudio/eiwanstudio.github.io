About us

Eiwan Studio is a small independent studio making friendly games and apps for players and families. In traditional Arab architecture, an "eiwan" is an open, welcoming arch: a warm space where people gather, share stories and connect. That is the spirit behind everything we build. We craft original, high-quality experiences for phones and tablets, rooted in our culture and designed with Arabic first. Whether you are testing your wits in Brain Fiesta, discovering local workshops on Eiwan Maharah, or making your child the hero of their own book in My Tale, you are always welcome here.
