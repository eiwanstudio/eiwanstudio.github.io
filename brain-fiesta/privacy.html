Privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what the Brain Fiesta app ("the app"), made by Eiwan Studio ("we"), collects and why. Contact: eiwanstudio.dev@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: a player ID created when you start as a guest, plus the player name and avatar you choose. If you choose to sign in with Google, we also receive your Google account email address, name and account ID, used only to sign you in and keep your progress safe across devices. We never show your email to other players, and we never ask for your phone number.
  • Game data: scores, levels, coins, Crystals, items, missions, friends list, match results and the preset reactions you send. There is no free text chat.
  • Player reports: if you report another player, we keep the report (who reported whom, the reason you pick and the match) so our team can review it.
  • Purchases: Google Play sends us a purchase token and order details so we can deliver what you bought. We never see your card details.
  • Crash and error reports: the platform (Android or iOS), app version, error text and your player ID, used to find and fix bugs.
  • Optional usage data (only if you agree): it is off until you say yes, and it is never offered to players under 13 or to anyone who has not answered the age question. If you agree, we record which screens you open and how many seconds you spend on each (for game screens also the game, mode and stage), and which games you play in online matches. It is linked to your player ID, contains no messages or personal details, and you can switch it off at any time in Settings.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. We only request non-personalised ads. AdMob may still collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not ask for photos, contacts or location.

How we use it

  • To run your account, save your progress and let you play with friends.
  • To deliver purchases and prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To fix crashes and improve the games, and, only if you agreed, to see which games and screens players enjoy.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

Where it is stored

Game and account data is stored with our database provider Supabase on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Purchases are handled by Google Play and ads by Google AdMob under their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as purchase records). Crash reports are kept only as long as needed to fix the problem. Optional usage data is kept for up to 12 months and is deleted with your account.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app or by email: see Delete account.
  • Ask for a copy or correction of your data by emailing us.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Children

Brain Fiesta is made for a mixed audience of all ages, including children under 13. When the app's age question shows a player is under 13, we only show ads that follow Google's rules for children (all ads in the app are non-personalised), we never collect usage data from them, and we collect only what is needed to run the game. Parents can ask us to see or delete their child's data at any time by emailing eiwanstudio.dev@gmail.com.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and change the date above.
