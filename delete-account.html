Delete your Brain Fiesta account

In the app

  1. Make sure your device is connected to the internet.
  2. Open Brain Fiesta and go to Settings, then the Help tab.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

The button only appears when the app is online.

By email

If you can't open the app, email eiwanstudio.dev@gmail.com from the email linked to your account, or include your player name and player ID (shown in your profile). We may ask you to confirm the request.

What is deleted

Your account, player name, avatar, progress, coins, Crystals, items, friends and match history are deleted within 30 days. Purchase records we must keep by law are kept for the required period. Crystals and purchases can't be refunded or restored after deletion.
