Privacy policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what the Brain Fiesta app ("the app"), made by Eiwan Studio ("we"), collects and why. Contact: eiwanstudio.dev@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: a player ID created when you start as a guest, or your Google account email and name if you sign in with Google. Your player name and avatar.
  • Game data: scores, levels, coins, Crystals, items, missions, friends list, match results and the preset reactions you send. There is no free text chat.
  • Purchases: Google Play sends us a purchase token and order details so we can deliver what you bought. We never see your card details.
  • Crash and error reports: device model, OS and app version and the error, kept for up to 90 days to fix bugs.
  • Advertising: the app shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. See how Google uses data in ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not ask for photos, contacts or location.

How we use it

  • To run your account, save your progress and let you play with friends.
  • To deliver purchases and prevent cheating and fraud.
  • To fix crashes and improve the games.
  • To show ads that keep the app free.

Where it is stored

Game and account data is stored with our database provider Supabase on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (European Union). Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Purchases are handled by Google Play and ads by Google AdMob under their own policies.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data while your account exists. When you delete your account we delete your personal data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (such as purchase records). Crash reports are kept up to 90 days.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account in the app or by email: see Delete account.
  • Ask for a copy or correction of your data by emailing us.
  • Change ad personalisation in your device or Google settings, and in the consent message where shown.

Children

The app is made for a general audience. If you believe a child has given us personal data without a parent's permission, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and change the date above.
